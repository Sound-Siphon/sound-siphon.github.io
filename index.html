How It Works

Step 1: Install and Launch

Run Sound Siphon. It appears as an audio device in System Settings and your apps.

Step 2: Select Sources

Choose apps or system audio to capture. Fine-tune levels and channels individually.

Step 3: Route to Destination

In your target app—like OBS, Logic, or Zoom—pick Sound Siphon as the input microphone.

Step 4: Capture or Stream

Audio flows instantly. Monitor, mute, or mix as needed. Done.
